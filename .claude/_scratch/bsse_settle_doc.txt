== MARCH 30 ISOMETRIC RESPONSE ==

Isometric strongly recommends that suppliers provide photo and visual evidence of third-party mixing, but this is not a mandatory requirement. Per section 8.7, projects are only required to supply the documentation listed in sections 8.7.1 (affidavits or declarations) and 8.7.2 (sales documentation). Any additional visual evidence provided would be considered supplementary.

The same position applies to weighbridge records. Isometric strongly recommends their inclusion, but they are not required.

Charm's proposal to provide certified scale tickets (loaded and unloaded) or a bill of lading satisfies the proof of delivery requirement under section 8.7.2. To fully satisfy this section, sales invoices or transfer records that clearly link the biochar volume and batch IDs to the purchaser would also be needed alongside this.

Charm's proposed affidavit goes a long way toward satisfying section 8.7.1, but two additional items are required that do not appear to be covered in the current proposal: (1) evidence that the receiving company has been an active agricultural or adjacent operation for at least 3 years, and (2) confirmation of the timeframe within which the biochar will be mixed following receipt. Both are explicitly required under section 8.7.1 of the module.
