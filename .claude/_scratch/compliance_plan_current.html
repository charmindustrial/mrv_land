BSSE v1.3 Compliance Plan — Biochar Vertical Handoff

Project: Charm Industrial  |  Module: Biochar Storage in Soil Environments v1.3  |  Date: May 2026  |  Prepared by: Max Lavine / MRV

How to Read This Plan

This plan covers the compliance work Charm must complete under Isometric's Biochar Storage in Soil Environments module before the next verification. Two teams share responsibility:

  • Biochar Vertical — operational changes, record-keeping, and coordination tasks your team owns
  • Max / MRV — Isometric submissions, PDD drafting, and verifier-facing documentation that MRV authors on behalf of the project

Throughout this document, items where the module uses the words must or required are mandatory with no exceptions. Items the module marks as should, recommended, or may are best practices only, not mandatory. Each requirement below is flagged with a Mandatory or Recommended badge accordingly.

This plan covers both commercial pathways: direct field spreading and third-party compost sales.

1. Biochar Characterization

Owner: Biochar Vertical

Every batch submitted for credit must carry a measured H/Corg ratio below 0.5. Isometric has confirmed this threshold is fixed — no exception is available.

Each batch must also carry a complete certified analytical package before submission: total organic carbon, hydrogen, volatile matter, ash, moisture, and the full heavy metals and contaminants panel from the module's characterization tables.

Action — Biochar Vertical: Confirm all lab contracts and sample submission procedures cover every required parameter. Any batch shipped without a complete certified analysis cannot be credited.

2. Stockpile Management

Owner: Biochar Vertical (operational) | Max / MRV (PDD and Isometric exemption note)

2a. Storage Duration Mandatory

"Biochar must not be stored for more than 12 months after production, unless otherwise agreed upon with Isometric." BSSE v1.3 §7.3

Charm has secured a 24-month window. The April 27 settlement document states: "Extension of the default duration to a 24-month window." The same document separately states: "Char that was produced during or before April 2026 will be exempted from storage timeline requirements. This was produced prior to Charm initiating verifications under biochar storage in soil environments v1.2."

Action — Biochar Vertical: Track production dates for every batch in inventory. Flag any batch approaching 24 months from its production date. Do not ship or apply a batch beyond the agreed window without advance coordination with Max.

2b. Weatherproof Cover Mandatory

"Be stored in a covered environment, ideally indoors, but at minimum under a secure, weatherproof cover that protects the material from rain, wind, and other environmental conditions." BSSE v1.3 §7.3

The March 30 Isometric response states: "we agree that Charm's current practice, storage in tied-off supersacks in an outdoor staging area, satisfies the module's weatherproof cover provision. No further action is needed on this point."

No change to current practice required.

2c. Wetting — Conditional Exemption Mandatory (exemption in place)

"Be stored in a wet condition or mixed with moist compost to minimize the risk of self-ignition." BSSE v1.3 §7.3

Isometric granted a conditional exemption. The April 17 Isometric response states: "Isometric will issue an exemption note for Charm's project in relation to the wetting requirement, on the basis that Charm provides a suitable proposal for storage in the absence of wetting."

The exemption applies to Charm's currently qualified char product (Fauske isothermal basket test returned a negative result). For all future products, the April 27 settlement states: "every new product (defined by a feedstock and pyrolysis technology combination) will be evaluated for self-heating using an industry-recognized method before being placed into stockpile storage. The corresponding storage plan will be set in concert with Charm's EHS Manager and made available for Isometric/VVB approval prior to use."

Action — Biochar Vertical: Before adding any new feedstock-pyrolysis combination to the pipeline, trigger a self-heating evaluation and share results with Max before initiating stockpile storage for that product.
Action — Max / MRV: Document Fauske test result, EHS-0045 SOP, and storage plan in the PDD to substantiate the exemption. Obtain the Isometric exemption note at next verification.

2d. Waterway Setbacks Recommended

"Be stored at a location that avoids proximity to waterway such as rivers, streams, or drainage areas where runoff could result in biochar loss." BSSE v1.3 §7.3
"Biochar should not be stored or spread within 10 meters from any watercourse and 50 meters from any spring, well or borehole." BSSE v1.3 §5.2.2.1
Action — Biochar Vertical: Confirm all current and planned storage sites meet both distance conditions. Provide site locations to Max / MRV for PDD documentation.

3. Site Selection for Direct Spreading

Owner: Biochar Vertical (site assessment) | Max / MRV (PDD documentation)

3a. Hard Prohibition — Steep Slopes Mandatory

"Biochar must not be spread on steep slopes where there is a significant risk of loss through erosion, unless evidence can be provided to show sufficient preventative measures to protect against erosion." BSSE v1.3 §5.2.2.1

No spreading on steep slopes without documented erosion controls reviewed by MRV in advance.

3b. Recommended Avoidance Conditions Recommended

The module specifies conditions under which spreading should not occur, including land that "Has been frozen for 12 hours or more in the preceding 24 hours" and land that "Is waterlogged, frozen or covered in snow." These are best-practice conditions — not hard prohibitions — but spreading under them without documented justification creates audit risk.

Action — Biochar Vertical: Include the steep-slope prohibition and the recommended avoidance conditions in the written guidance or purchase agreement provided to each farmer customer. Keep a record when application occurs near any avoidance condition.

4. Evidence for Direct Soil Application

Owner: Biochar Vertical (records) | Max / MRV (Certify submission)
"Project Proponents must document either of the following methods detailed in Section 8.5.1 or 8.5.2 that are accepted as sufficient evidence of spreading of biochar:" BSSE v1.3 §8.5

Charm has elected the project boundary mapping and application records pathway only. This was confirmed with Isometric in the April 27 settlement. Geotagged photos are not required.

4a. Project Boundary Maps Mandatory

"High-resolution maps or GIS layers clearly delineating the area of land where biochar application occurred. If the landowner requests anonymity, proof can be provided at the ZIP code (or equivalent) level." BSSE v1.3 §8.5.2
"Spreading is not permitted outside of the project boundaries agreed in the PDD." BSSE v1.3 §8.5.2
Action — Biochar Vertical: For each spreading location, provide Max / MRV with GPS coordinates or a street address and confirm field boundaries before the first application. Spreading outside a pre-agreed boundary requires advance coordination with MRV to update the PDD.

4b. Application Records Mandatory

"Dates of application and the quantity of biochar (e.g., in tonnes) applied to each specific area evidenced by weighbridge or inventory records or affidavit, from which an application rate can be calculated." BSSE v1.3 §8.5.2
Action — Biochar Vertical: For each application event, record: (a) date or date range, (b) quantity in tonnes, (c) acreage covered. For prior agreements where specific dates were not recorded at signing (e.g., Brenner), back-fill application dates and acreage from grower records confirmed via written attestation from the offtaker.
Action — Biochar Vertical (ongoing): Update the standard sales agreement template to include application date (or estimated date range) and spreading acreage as required fields on all future agreements.

4c. Best Practice Guidance for Farmer Customers Mandatory

"For application direct to soil Project Proponents must provide offtakers or downstream receivers with a best practice guidance for handling and application to ensure the risk of harm to the environment and human health is minimized." BSSE v1.3 §8.2.1
Action — Max / MRV: Create a best practice guidance document for farmer customers covering safe handling, application conditions (including the steep-slope prohibition), and environmental protections. Deliver it to every direct-spread customer before or at time of first sale.

5. Evidence for Third-Party Compost Sales

Owner: Biochar Vertical (records and affidavit logistics) | Max / MRV (Certify submission and affidavit template)
"Project Proponents must document all of the following methods detailed in Section 8.7.1 and 8.7.2:" BSSE v1.3 §8.7

Photos and weighbridge records are not mandatory under this pathway. The March 30 Isometric response states: "Isometric strongly recommends that suppliers provide photo and visual evidence of third-party mixing, but this is not a mandatory requirement." On weighbridge records, the same response states: "The same position applies to weighbridge records. Isometric strongly recommends their inclusion, but they are not required."

5a. Purchaser Affidavit Mandatory — §8.7.1

"As well as at least 3 years of evidence that the company receiving the biochar is an active agricultural or adjacent company." BSSE v1.3 §8.7.1
"The biochar processing facility will mix the biochar with the material, to reduce the risk of reversal, within a stated number of days of receipt of the biochar. Credits will not be issued against this biochar until this time period during which mixing should occur has elapsed." BSSE v1.3 §8.7.1

The March 30 Isometric response states that Charm's existing affidavit is missing: "two additional items are required that do not appear to be covered in the current proposal: (1) evidence that the receiving company has been an active agricultural or adjacent operation for at least 3 years, and (2) confirmation of the timeframe within which the biochar will be mixed following receipt."

Action — Max / MRV: Update the affidavit template to add both required items. Template must be finalized before the next reporting period opens.
Action — Biochar Vertical: Obtain a signed, updated affidavit from every third-party compost customer before biochar ships. No affidavit = no credit for that batch.
Action — Biochar Vertical: For each new compost customer, gather 3-year operating history documentation (business registration records, permit history, or equivalent).

5b. Sales Documentation Mandatory — §8.7.2

"Sales invoices, purchase orders, or transfer records that clearly link the volume and unique batch IDs of the biochar sold to the specific purchaser." BSSE v1.3 §8.7.2

The module also requires proof of delivery documentation including "Bills of lading (BOLs)" and "Delivery receipts signed by the recipient, or proof of delivery."

Action — Biochar Vertical: For every compost customer shipment, the record package must include: (a) a sales invoice with the batch ID and quantity, and (b) a signed BOL or delivery receipt. Certified scale tickets are also acceptable in place of a delivery receipt. Forward to MRV at or before verification submission.

6. Chain of Custody

Owner: Biochar Vertical (batch IDs and handoff docs) | Max / MRV (diagram and PDD)
"Project Proponents must provide a clear chain of custody diagram or equivalent." BSSE v1.3 §8.9

6a. Chain of Custody Diagram Mandatory

A diagram or equivalent document tracing each batch from production through to end use must be included in the PDD.

Action — Max / MRV: Create a chain-of-custody diagram covering both pathways (direct spreading and third-party compost sales) for inclusion in the PDD before next verification.

6b. Unique Batch IDs on All Documentation Mandatory

"Every batch of biochar produced must be assigned a unique, sequential identification number. This ID must be used on all related documentation." BSSE v1.3 §8.9
Action — Biochar Vertical: Confirm every batch in inventory has a unique sequential ID and that this ID appears consistently on all outgoing documentation — production logs, lab reports, sales invoices, BOLs, and delivery receipts.

6c. Documentation at Every Handoff Mandatory

The module requires documentation at every point where custody changes hands, citing dispatch notes, receiving reports, signed delivery notes, bills of lading, and invoices as adequate evidence.

Action — Biochar Vertical: For every batch transfer — to a direct-spread customer or a compost facility — retain at least one of the listed documents, signed and dated.

6d. Five-Year Record Retention Mandatory

"All chain-of-custody documentation, along with all other evidence, must be retained for a minimum period (5 years from the point of creation). Records should be stored securely and be readily accessible for audit." BSSE v1.3 §8.9
Action — Biochar Vertical + Max / MRV: All chain-of-custody records must be kept for at least 5 years from creation and stored so they can be retrieved quickly for audit.
