SAF certified this way will only carry the CORSIA claim, and will not benefit from the additional sustainability impact and claims provided by achieving full RSB CORSIA certification.\n\nAre you ready to apply for RSB CORSIA Certification?\nApply for certification\nRSB Academy's e-learning course\n\nAre you a fuel producer, auditor, or policymaker working with SAF?
