It comprises various procedures and methodologies that describe how to produce and trade bio-based and advanced fuels.\n\nExplore the Standards & Procedures for this scheme\nCertification context\n\nThis scheme enables the certification of fuels made from:\n\nPrimary biomass\nBiomass from end-of-life products and production residues\nFossil-based end-of-life products\nRenewable liquid and gaseous fuels of non-biological origin\nCo-processing biogenic with fossil fuels\n\nRSB’s Global Fuels Certification is to be used by biomass producers, fuel producers, traders, processors and transporters in any region of the world – excluding those trading in the EU regulated market.\n\nAre you ready to apply for RSB Global Fuels Certification?\nApply for certification\nRSB Academy's e-learning course\n\nThe RSB Academy’s course on RSB Global Fuels Certification is designed for stakeholders in the fuels sector, and outlines the requirements and processes for obtaining RSB’s Global Fuels Certification. Participants will learn about sustainable production standards specific to fuels, gaining knowledge to ensure compliance and benefit from certification.\n\nStart your e-learning journey\nWorking with the RSB Principles & Criteria\n\nWhen it comes to working with RSB’s sustainability framework, not every document applies to every producer. By completing the self-risk assessment and screening exercises before the certification process, your context-specific sustainability requirements will be set out.\n\nDepending on your certification scope and the type of operator you are, you will only need to work with some of these documents.
