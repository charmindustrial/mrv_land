AECN EMBODIED CARBON INTENSITY — EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT, MAINTENANCE, SPARE PARTS AND CONSUMABLES
Response to verifier query
15 September 2026


QUESTION

The AECN embodied emissions CI has not changed in two years. Please clarify whether AECN
is accounting for any equipment replacement, maintenance, spare parts or consumables in the
CI, and how that is being done or tracked. If not, please request an updated embodied
emissions CI to be calculated for each year half, or demonstrate that it is not material.


RESPONSE

No. The embodied figure covers the one-off cost of building the plant — equipment,
construction, installation — spread over lifetime output. Nothing in it covers parts or
supplies bought while the plant runs, and nothing is tracked period to period. It is fixed
by design, which is why it hasn't moved.

We don't think it needs recalculating. Taking the oil as 40% carbon by mass (our assumption,
not an AECN life-cycle figure — a higher true value helps this case), a tonne of oil locks
away 1.47 tonnes of CO2. Subtracting AECN's own emissions of roughly 0.12 tonnes from
production and 0.021 embodied leaves 1.32 tonnes of net removal per tonne of oil. One percent
of that is 13 kg of CO2. At the US EPA's published factor for industrial machinery repair and
maintenance, a dollar of spending carries about 0.13 kg — so it takes about USD 100 of spending
per tonne of oil produced to give up those 13 kg. That is CAD 143 at today's rate, and across
AECN's output of roughly 25,000 tonnes of oil a year it comes to CAD 3.6 million a year before
it made a 1% difference — more than 3% of the plant's build cost, every year, on parts and
supplies.

As a check, our own pyrolysis site records this spending directly: USD 1,767 per pyrolyzer per
month, about USD 21,200 a year per machine. Our site is harder on parts than Port-Cartier,
since we keep swapping out pyrolyzer generations, and it still sits orders of magnitude below
the threshold.

Proposal: leave the embodied figure as it stands. If you would prefer plant-specific numbers,
we can ask AECN to report annual maintenance and consumables spend with each half-yearly
submission.
