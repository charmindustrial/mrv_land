Grid region is set by the balancing authority a site connects to, not by state. The federal generator database (EIA Form 860M, July 2026) lists Wildflower Solar, plant 66369, with balancing authority MISO. Basco is on MISO too: it buys its power from Cleco, and the same database shows Cleco's Coughlin plant in Evangeline Parish, Basco's own parish, on MISO. MISO's Louisiana and Mississippi zones are 9 and 10 (MISO tariff Attachment VV), both on MISO's South side, which Table 5 of the DOE 45VH2-GREET manual maps to Delta. Both sites are therefore in Delta.
