# Monthly Request Email — Template

Sent automatically by the Requester on the 1st of each month from **g_mrv@charmindustrial.com** to all reporters. One message per reporter (so each gets their own subject tag and instructions), or a single message to all three — the per-reporter version is below because it lets each person reply on a thread that already carries the right tag.

`{MONTH}` / `{YEAR}` = the reporting period just completed (e.g., a request sent 2026-07-01 asks for June 2026 reporting). `{REPORTING_PERIOD}` = e.g. "June 2026".

---

## To Alison — Community Engagement Log

**To:** alison@charmindustrial.com
**Subject:** `[Community]` MRV reporting request — {REPORTING_PERIOD}

Hi Alison,

It's time for the {REPORTING_PERIOD} MRV reporting. Please reply to this email with the Community Engagement reporting for the period:

- Updated Events List, and
- Updated Grievance Record

(one file or two — both are needed). Keeping the `[Community]` tag in the subject helps it get filed automatically. Thanks!

— MRV Bot

## Reminder messages

Sent at **3** and then **5 business days** after the request to any reporter not yet received. Same thread as the original request.

**3-business-day reminder:**

> Hi {NAME}, a friendly nudge — we haven't received your {REPORTING_PERIOD} {DATA_TYPE} yet. Whenever you get a chance, just reply here with it. Thanks!

**5-business-day reminder:**

> Hi {NAME}, following up again on the {REPORTING_PERIOD} {DATA_TYPE} — we're still missing it and it's holding up the period's reporting. Could you send it over today? Happy to help if anything's in the way. Thanks!
