Bio-Oil Transport / Transport Process Emissions -- Method 1, 'Emission Calculation Method' cell:
"Transport process emissions are computed using the \"Distance-Based Method\" outlined in Section 3.3
of the Transportation Emissions Accounting Module. For a given journey, the mass of cargo is multiplied
by the journey distance and the product is multiplied by a Well-to-Wheel emission factor for the
appropriate vehicle type used for the journey."

Same row, 'Monitoring/Data Collection Method' cell:
"Distance: The route between origin and destination will be set using Google maps, selecting the
longest route if multiple are presented"

Same row, 'Emission Factors Used' cell:
"Distance-based emission factors from GLEC V3 Section 3 Module 2 will be used unless otherwise noted"

Bio-Oil Production Process Emissions (Charm), 'Data Collection Method' cell:
"Mass: Charm bio-oil is currently transported in totes -- Once filled, totes are weighted on a
calibrated scale and the weight is recorded along with a corresponding UID number. Oil mass is
estimated as the gross weight of the tote less its tare weight"
