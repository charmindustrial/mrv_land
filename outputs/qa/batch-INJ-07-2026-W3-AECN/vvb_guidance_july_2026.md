## Trailer Standard Empty Weight (AECN Rail Offloads)
Purpose: "For AECN rail offloads, net bio-oil mass injected is the Full scale weight minus the Empty scale weight of the same tractor + trailer. When an offload's empty scale ticket is missing, or its trailer/tractor does not match the Full ticket (a rig mismatch), the recorded empty weight cannot be used directly. In these cases a conservative per-trailer standard empty weight is substituted."
Method: "For each trailer, empty (tare) weights were compiled from all available CAT Scale empty tickets across the April–July 2026 rail offloads (n = 48 valid weighings). The conservative standard empty weight is defined as the per-trailer mean plus one standard deviation. Because a heavier empty weight yields a lower net injected mass, mean + 1 sigma ensures the substitution is conservative (under-crediting)..."
Standard empty weights (as of July 2026): KL192 n=22 mean 29,321 sigma 189 standard 29,510 lb | KL188 n=14 mean 28,997 sigma 309 standard 29,307 lb | KL720 n=8 mean 29,515 sigma 130 standard 29,645 lb | KL510 n=4 mean 28,985 sigma 493 standard 29,478 lb
Trigger: "For a given offload, substitute the standard empty weight for the trailer recorded on the Full ticket when either (a) the empty scale ticket is missing, or (b) the empty ticket's tractor and/or trailer do not match the Full ticket. Substituted values are annotated in the ops-notes Empty Scale Ticket cell."
Application list present in the doc: "Application — July 1 – July 31 RP (Week 1, INJ-07-2026-W1)" only — four GRPX5188/GPRX5107 offloads.
