AECN Rail — Basis for Loaded Mass (Week 3, INJ-09-2026-W3)
Scope. The basis described here applies to Week 3 of this Reporting Period, INJ-09-2026-W3, covering 11 – 18 September 2026, and to that week alone. For all other weeks in this Reporting Period, the mass loaded at origin is taken from the metric-tons figure stated on the Bill of Lading.
Method. For railcar shipments of AECN third-party bio-oil in that week, the mass loaded at origin is derived from the volume recorded on the Bill of Lading and a bio-oil density of 1.22 kg/L:
Loaded mass (kg) = BOL volume at 60°F (L) × 1.22 kg/L
The volume at 60°F is taken from the Product Shipment Summary on each Bill of Lading; a railcar is loaded by three shipments and its loaded mass is the sum of the three.
Offloaded mass and the balance. Mass offloaded at the injection site is measured independently, by certified truck scale, as the difference between full and empty weights for each offload. Sparging and filtration losses are deducted from the offloaded mass to give the Mass of Bio-Oil Injected, which is subtracted directly from the railcar’s loaded mass. Loaded mass and offloaded mass are therefore established by two independent methods, and the ops notes present both together so the balance for each railcar can be inspected directly.
Railcars offloaded across more than one week. Where a railcar is offloaded across more than one reporting week, the loaded mass is recorded once, in the week the railcar’s first offload occurs, and subsequent weeks draw against the same shipment.
