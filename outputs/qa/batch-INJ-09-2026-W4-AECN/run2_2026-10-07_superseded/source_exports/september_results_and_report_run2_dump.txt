58 : Charm-Produced Bio-Oil Injections - Production Period MRV
59 : Charm's integration of dMRV functionality for our CO production facility is not yet complete at the time of this report because of ongoing technical discussions with Isometric's digestion of the data. Emissions for Charm oil have been computed and reported using the same spreadsheet-based method as outlined in the previous GHG Statement Report. Any product sequestered in this period has an associated data package detailing the carbon accounting and how emissions are allocated to that product. All product produced during H2 2026 is accounted for using the H1 2026 CI, following the cadence introduced in a prior reporting period.
61 : Standard Bio-Oil Mass Deduction for Mass Lost to Sparging
62 : Because sparged oil is no longer offloaded into trucks and weighed prior to injection, Charm's Materials Science team performed a set of benchtop experiments, sparging bio-oil with set points designed to mimic the conditions of oil being sparged in bulk. Based on the results of these trials, an average mass loss of 1.29% was observed across multiple samples. For all oil sparged on site, 1.29% of the input mass is deducted from the MOBI to account for the loss of light compounds during the sparging process.
64 : Inventory Amortization
65 : As introduced in the August 2026 Reporting Period, Charm amortizes embodied emissions inventory using a single combined asset pool spanning all Charm locations. The amortization rate is based on gross CDR as a proportion of the gross CDR expected over the project lifetime per Emissions Amortization option 3 given in Section 7 of GHG Accounting v1.1, and will be updated at at least the prescribed 1, 3, and 5 year cadence as variances between projections and actuals are realized.
67 : Rolling Six-Month Carbon Intensity for Charm Production Emissions
68 : Charm applies a rolling six-month mass-weighted carbon intensity (CI) as the production-side embodied-emissions factor. For production from July 2026 onward the fallback CI is derived from the January-June 2026 window, mass-weighted across all production in that window.
70 : MapBox Mapping Software
71 : In addition to Google Maps, some distance measurements are routed using Mapbox software. Mapbox is a large-scale commercially available mapping software comparable in function to Google Maps and used extensively in high sensitivity applications such as geospatial services and onboard vehicle navigation. Regardless of the software used, where multiple routing options are given, the longest will be used.
73 : AECN Rail - Oil Withdrawn for Research and Development (Week 3)
74 : On 12 September 2026, 200 US gallons of AECN bio-oil were drawn from the Week 3 aggregate AECN oil and shipped to Charm's Colorado facility for equipment testing. This oil was not injected during this Reporting Period and is excluded from the Week 3 MOBI. The volume is converted to mass at the SGS-measured Week 3 composite density of 1.192 kg/L (200 gal x 3.785411784 L/gal x 1.192 kg/L = 902.44 kg), reducing Week 3 AECN rail MOBI from 194,394.38 kg to 193,491.94 kg. Full detail is provided in the VVB Guidance September 1 - September 30 document.
76 : AECN Rail - New Rail Destination at Baton Rouge (Week 4)
77 : Beginning 22 September 2026, AECN railcars are routed to a transload rail spur in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, instead of the Opelousas rail yard, with the oil transferred to tanker trucks and hauled by road to the injection site. One railcar (SHPX215053, Week 4) used the new route in this period. Its rail leg (2,152 mi) and tanker leg (136 mi one way, counted as a round trip) are calculated and reported separately from the Opelousas railcars, each on that railcar's own mass and route distance. From the next Reporting Period, Baton Rouge is the standard destination for AECN rail deliveries.
79 : AECN Rail - Bio-oil Lost Between Loading and Delivery
80 : Pyrolysis and transport components on each AECN rail removal are calculated on delivered mass (certified scale-ticket net mass, less any Uncertainty Adjustment). The difference between loaded mass (loading Bills of Lading) and delivered mass is quantified per railcar, recognised in the week the railcar's final offload completes, and reported as a separate component, AECN Bio-oil Loss, using the same AECN pyrolysis carbon intensities and loading-origin-to-rail-yard transport emissions pro-rated to the lost mass. In this period 1,373.38 kg was lost across ten railcars, carrying 0.315 t CO2e.
82 : Site Gasoline - E-85 Emission Factor
83 : Five of the seven site-vehicle fuel fills this period were dispensed as E-85. These are quantified at an E-85 well-to-wheel emission factor derived from Argonne National Laboratory's R&amp;D GREET 2025 Revision 1, calculated at the minimum ASTM D5798 ethanol content of 51% (2.4701 kg CO2e/kg fuel). Conventional gasoline fills remain at 3.74 kg CO2e/kg fuel (GLEC Framework v3.2).
85 : Site Diesel - Rail-Yard Air Compressor Fuel
86 : Diesel used by the towable air compressor that offloads AECN railcars at the rail yard is now drawn from the offload tanker trucks, so it is not captured by fuel receipts. It is estimated as rail offloads x 2 h per offload x 2.4 US gal/h (manufacturer full-load rate): 40 offloads x 2 h x 2.4 gal/h = 192 US gal, converted at 3.22 kg/gal and 3.87 kg CO2e/kg (GLEC Framework v3.2) to 2,392.59 kg CO2e.
