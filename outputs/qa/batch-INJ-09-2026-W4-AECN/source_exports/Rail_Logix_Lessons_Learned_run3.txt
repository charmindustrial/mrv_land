Ideas for Improvement
Procedural Ideas
* Reduce railcar circulation
   * Move to a 15-minute circulation, mixes it a little bit, maybe not entirely
* Eliminate railcar circulation
   * If we get a full railcar a day, we can circulate in the Frac tank instead
* Prove out accuracy of Bluetooth weight sensor and/or coriolis flow meter
   * Eliminates the need to get scale tickets, not sure how much time this saves


Staffing Ideas
* Move to 14-hour shifts, 3-on/4-off
   * Drivers would get 42 hours per week
   * A Shift M-W, B Shift Thu-Sat, Everyone off on Sunday
   * Sunday would be OT offered to catch up if railcars are piling up
   * Supports 6x railcars per week base
   * Fixes the inconsistency of paychecks with the 4-on 4-off schedule
* Move to two 8-hour shifts, M-F
   * Drivers would get 40 hours per week
   * A shift, 4AM - 12PM, gets the first load
   * B shift, 12PM - 8PM, gets the second load
   * Saturday and Sunday would be OT offered to catch up if railcars are piling up
   * Supports 5x railcars per week base
   * Fixes the inconsistency of paychecks with the 4-on 4-off schedule


Equipment Ideas
* Bigger pump or 2 pump skids, reduces offload time
   * Note: also increases risk of overfill
* Multiple trailers
   * Enables a drop-and-go method where full trailers are dropped off and empty trailers are picked up, reducing on-site time at Basco
* Diesel tanks on-site at Basco
   * Eliminates need to fill-up on-the-road
   * Not sure how much time this saves
