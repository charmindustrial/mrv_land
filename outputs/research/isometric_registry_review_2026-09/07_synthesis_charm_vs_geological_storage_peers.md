# Charm vs the geological-storage peers on the Isometric registry — synthesis and opportunity register

Prepared 2026-09-14 for Max Lavine (Charm MRV lead). Internal decision document.

**How to read this.** Every quotation below is carried forward word-for-word from one of the six input files in this folder (`01_registry_census.md`, `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md`, `03_peer_nulife_ontario_avenue.md`, `04_peer_vaulted_great_plains.md`, `05_peer_vaulted_tire.md`, `06_charm_registry_footprint.md`), or from the public Isometric "Bio-oil Geological Storage" protocol pages (v1.0 and v1.2) read in the headless browser this session. Page numbers are the ones the input files give (PDF page numbers of the underlying document). Where a number is arithmetic on quoted figures it says "arithmetic". Where the sources do not settle a point it says "not determinable from the sources read". Nothing in this document is estimated.

Short glossary (plain words for the terms that keep coming up): **PDD** = Project Design Document, the long form a project files once describing how it works. **GHG Statement Report** = the short form filed with every batch of removals sent for verification. **Verification report** = the independent checker's write-up. **Datapoint** = one input number shown on the registry's public calculation page for a removal. **Method A / Method B** = the protocol's two options for how often bio-oil carbon content is lab-tested (every batch, or a sample of batches with a conservative estimate for the rest). **Materiality** = the size below which a small emission may be left out.

## 7. Open questions that need a protocol read or an Isometric conversation

**Q1 — Does Charm meet the protocol's minimum number of samples per batch?** This is the sharpest finding of the review and it cuts against Charm, not for it. NULIFE reads §7.4.1.1.1 as requiring three: "A minimum of 3 samples are taken per HTL production batch, as per the minimum samples per batch requirements in Section 7.4.1.1.1." (p20). Charm reads the same section as one: "Per section 7.4.1.1.1 of the Bio-Oil Geological Storage protocol, a sample is taken for each injection batch" and "one sample will be sent to an analytic lab to measure the carbon content of that batch." The protocol text (v1.0 §7.4.1.1.1, read this session on the registry) says both things, in two places:

> "A minimum of one sample per Injection Batch (for blended injections) or one per Production Batch (for non-blended injections) of bio-oil must be collected and analyzed. Samples shall be from a well mixed and representative aliquot of the bio-oil as injected, insuring that solids are blended in the sample and representative of the amount in the injectant."

and, under the sub-heading "Minimum number of samples per Batch", which Method A points to ("For the acceptable minimum number of samples to take per sampled Batch, see Minimum number of samples per Batch below. If multiple samples are taken per Batch, the average C content of these samples must be used."):

> "For all measurements taken, samples must be from a well mixed and representative aliquot of the injectant. To account for the possibility of variation within a single Production Batch (for example within a large container of the injectant), either of the following approaches must be adopted:
> A minimum of 3 samples must be taken for each measured Production Batch.
> Justification and evidence must be provided to demonstrate that the "within batch" variation is likely to be minimal. For example, this could be justified due to the physical details of the Production Process used, or alternatively by providing data examining the "within batch" C content variation."

The current v1.2 carries the identical wording, renumbered as §8.3.1.1 (verified this session; the v1.1 that Charm's verifier applies to AECN and Charm oil was not opened, so its numbering is not determinable). So one sample is compliant only if Charm has provided the "Justification and evidence… that the 'within batch' variation is likely to be minimal". Whether that justification exists is **not determinable from the sources read**: `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` quotes Charm's sampling text in full and it contains no such justification, and the profile's own "Cannot tell from the text" list does not mention one. Charm's verifier has passed seventeen rounds on this practice ("Meets the requirements of Isometric Standard V1.8 and Bio-oil Geological Storage Protocol v1.2 (Kerry Bio-oil), v1.1 (AECN and Charm Bio-oil) for Verification"), so either the justification was accepted outside the PDD text, or the point has not been raised. **Action:** find whether a within-batch variation justification was ever filed (sparging and homogenisation-tank mixing are the obvious "physical details of the Production Process"); if not, prepare one before a verifier or Isometric raises it. Register row 1 depends on the same section, so the two should be handled together.

**Q2 — Is Method B available to Charm under the bio-oil protocol?** Yes, on the protocol text: "This Protocol provides two alternative methods… the second method (B) involves only sampling some batches, and conservatively estimating the C content of unsampled batches." (v1.0 §7.4.1.1.1; v1.2 §8.3.1.1 identical). The questions that need Isometric are (i) what a "Production Batch" and a "Production Process" are for each of Charm's three oil streams — the protocol's own model (v1.2 §8.1) is "a 'Production Batch', p, which typically consists of utilizing a single type of biomass feedstock, often of a single source of origin"; Charm's PDD defines a monthly "Production Period" for Charm oil ("An example Production Period would be the month of January 2025"), so 30 Production Batches at that grain is 30 months; (ii) how a blended Injection Batch is scored when some constituent Production Batches are unsampled — Method A's route is "a linear weighted combination of the C contents of these Production Batches, as given in Equation 3", Method B's is Equation 5 "mean − standard error" per Production Process; (iii) the random-sampling arrangement: "Isometric will contact the Project Proponent on randomly selected days, at an agreed cadence, which must be no less frequent than once per month, on average" — Great Plains documented exactly this ("Isometric can contact the Great Plains Biomass Facility before 9am MT to request the first three batches that day to be sampled"). The 6-month eligibility window and the restart rule ("If there is a significant change to a Production Process for a feedstock… sampling must be restarted") matter for AECN, whose LCA and process are revised at least annually.

**Q3 — The v1.11 public / v1.12 internal PDD gap.** The registry shows "v1.11 – 2025/10/21", metadata "[Without Biochar Appendix]", 149 pages; Drive holds "v1.12 – 2025/12/01", ≥155 pages, with the Boulder Creek feedstock appendix and the biochar appendix added. Whether v1.12 was ever submitted to Isometric, and whether the public copy is deliberately the no-biochar build (the biochar project has its own registry page), is not determinable from the sources read. The verifier's July report applies "Biomass Feedstock Accounting Module v1.3 (Kerry Feedstock)" and the registry Feedstocks table lists "Kerry Inc 1-01 / Biomass / 12 Feb 2026" — Kerry has no appendix in either PDD version, so the public PDD is behind the verified project on at least that point. **Action:** confirm with Isometric which PDD version is the validated one, and whether a v1.13 with Kerry and the standard-loss rules (sparging 1.29%, filtration 0.071%) should be posted.

**Q4 — Materiality exclusions.** Which module section and table format NULIFE used ("Isometric GHG Accounting (V1) Materiality Assessment (Section 4)") and whether it applies under the GHG Accounting Module v1.1 the verifier lists for Kerry — needs the module read. Row 2.

**Q5 — Embodied amortisation.** v1.2 §8.5.1 defers to "Section 7 of the GHG Accounting Module"; Charm's FARs ISS-36/ISS-34 are already an open conversation.
