# NULIFE Ontario Avenue — what is public about the non-accredited carbon-content lab

Date of reading: 2026-09-14. Every quote below was read on that date from the file or page named. Where the documents are silent, this file says "not stated".

Question asked: NULIFE's PDD (page 20) says its carbon-content lab, the University of Saskatchewan Chemical Engineering Lab, is "deemed an in-house lab that is not ISO17025 accredited lab" and that "An In-house lab is deemed acceptable by Isometric by supplying more information. Attachment 'Carbon content lab test requirements v1.0' is provided". What exactly did NULIFE have to supply, and what does Isometric require of a lab that is not accredited?

Short answer up front: the attachment "Carbon content lab test requirements v1.0" is not published anywhere public that was found. The only public statements are (1) the protocol's one-sentence rule that the lab must hold "accreditation to ISO 17025 or equivalent standards for laboratory quality management for the specific test method", plus a three-item list of quality checks the lab must run, and (2) the verifier's one-sentence note that NULIFE "uses a lab that has provided the necessary details Isometric requested for non-ISO 17025 labs". Neither Isometric nor NULIFE publishes what those "necessary details" were.

### 7. Isometric Bio-oil Geological Storage protocol — the actual requirement
Source: page text captured 2026-09-14 from https://registry.isometric.com/protocol/bio-oil-geological-storage/1.2 (saved as `raw/protocol_bio_oil_geological_storage_v1.2_page_text_2026-09-14.txt`) and from the v1.0 page (saved as `raw/protocol_bio_oil_geological_storage_v1.0_page_text_2026-09-14.txt`).

v1.2, section 8.3.1.1 "Bio-oil Carbon Content Measurement":
- "The following test method should be used where possible, and must be used for bio-oils or blends with vapor pressures higher than 3 psi: NREL Laboratory Analysis Procedure for Determination of Carbon, Hydrogen, and Nitrogen in Bio-oils"
- "Where this is not possible, other acceptable test methods include: ASTM D5291: Standard Test Methods for Instrumental Determination of Carbon, Hydrogen, and Nitrogen in Petroleum Products and Lubricants / ASTM D5373: Standard Test Methods for Determination of Carbon, Hydrogen and Nitrogen in Analysis Samples of Coal and Carbon in Analysis Samples of Coal and Coke"
- "Alternative methods or analytical equipment which determine total carbon content may be utilized if justified and documented to be equivalent to ASTM D5291."
- "A minimum of one sample per Injection Batch (for blended injections) or one per Production Batch (for non-blended injections) of bio-oil must be collected and analyzed. Samples shall be from a well mixed and representative aliquot of the bio-oil as injected, insuring that solids are blended in the sample and representative of the amount in the injectant."
- "Analysis must be completed by a qualified laboratory, as evidenced by accreditation to ISO 17025 or equivalent standards for laboratory quality management for the specific test method (ASTM D5291)."
- "Laboratories shall complete standard quality assurance procedures on a schedule in accordance with their quality management plans and accreditation requirements to include: analysis of blanks / analysis of duplicates / instrumentation calibrations and analysis of calibration standards"

v1.0 carries the identical two sentences ("Analysis must be completed by a qualified laboratory, as evidenced by accreditation to ISO 17025 or equivalent standards for laboratory quality management for the specific test method (ASTM D5291)." and the same three-item quality list). v1.0's monitoring-plan table additionally has, for the carbon-content row, the columns: "ISO 17025 accredited laboratory" under the equipment/qualification column and "Analytical reports from qualified laboratory for audited samples, including supporting lab QA/QC results" under required evidence. A text search of the v1.2 capture for "ISO 17025 accredited laboratory" and "Analytical reports from qualified laboratory" returns no hit, so the v1.2 page as captured does not carry that table row wording (the v1.2 capture has 5 occurrences of "17025" in total; v1.0 has 10).

Neither protocol version defines the phrase "equivalent standards for laboratory quality management", nor uses the words "in-house". A text search of both captures for "in-house" returns nothing.

Other "17025" mentions in both versions are about different things: tail-gas emissions testers ("accredited to the Stack Testing Accreditation Council for ASTM D7036, ISO 17025, or approved by state regulatory authority"), flow-meter calibration ("Flow meters must be calibrated to national traceable standards by an ISO 17025 accredited metrology laboratory"), the RCRA hazardous-waste test ("testing completed by an accredited laboratory in accordance with U.S. EPA methods"), and the reference list entry "ISO/IEC 17025:2017 General requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories".

## (b) What the documents say NULIFE supplied or committed to (quoted items only)

1. The named lab and its status: "University of Saskatchewan Chemical Engineering Lab (deemed an in-house lab that is not ISO17025 accredited lab*)" (PDD p.20; repeated for the gas analysis on p.21).
2. An attachment, not public: "Attachment 'Carbon content lab test requirements v1.0' is provided for more details on the procedures of the equipment." (PDD p.20 and p.21).
3. "more information" to Isometric: "An In-house lab is deemed acceptable by Isometric by supplying more information." (PDD p.20). The verifier's plan was to "Confirm that the University of Saskatchewan Chemical Engineering Lab, an in-house lab, provides additional required information to Isometric for acceptability." (VVR p.30) and its conclusion was "NuLife uses a lab that has provided the necessary details Isometric requested for non-ISO 17025 labs." (VVR p.14; repeated Nov 2025 p.15).
4. Test method: "elemental analysis (ASTM D5921 or equivalent)" (PDD p.20).
5. Sampling: "A minimum of 3 samples are taken per HTL production batch", "Samples are taken from a well mixed allotment, including any solids", "Samples are dried in an oven, to remove moisture, before preparation for elemental analysis", "Samples are driven, bi-weekly, to the lab which is 6km away" (PDD p.20).
6. Duplicates: "Each sample is tested for carbon wt% in duplicates and the average of the two samples is utilized" (PDD p.20).
7. Re-run rule: "In the case of carbon outliers (variance between the duplicate samples), the lab technician is asked to re-run samples to re-assess. The variance is +/- 5 wt%" (PDD p.20).
8. Outlier flagging: "NULIFE's internal tracking program has a mechanism built in to flag carbon wt% outliers so they are rapidly identified." (PDD p.20).
9. Use of standards on the instrument: "high certainty due to the elemental analysis equipment operational requirements of utilizing standards to confirm accuracy of equipment" (PDD p.48).
10. Record retention: "Raw Bio-oil carbon wt% and tailgas GC analytical reports" are stored (PDD p.44); "Carbon wt% values ... are stored for at least 5 years" (PDD p.45).
11. Gap handling: where a run had no lab data, "the conservative value of 70% carbon is assumed, which is 5% less than the weighted average" (PDD p.48).
12. Per-batch evidence files the verifier reviewed: "Carbon Content Of Product Batch #021.xlsx", "#024", "#025", "#026", "#027" (Verification Report Aug 2026 pp.10, 23–24).

## (c) What is not stated anywhere public

- The contents of "Carbon content lab test requirements v1.0" — it is named in two PDD footnotes and nowhere else. It is not in the registry's 18-row document table, not reproduced in the PDD, and no web page with that title was found.
- What Isometric's "necessary details ... requested for non-ISO 17025 labs" actually are (VVR p.14). No verifier report, the PDD, the protocol, the salt-cavern module, or Isometric's documentation site lists them.
- Whether the University of Saskatchewan lab runs "analysis of blanks" or "instrumentation calibrations and analysis of calibration standards" (protocol §8.3.1.1). The PDD describes duplicates and a re-run rule; it says nothing about blanks or calibration standards beyond the phrase "utilizing standards to confirm accuracy of equipment" (PDD p.48).
- Whether the lab was judged against "equivalent standards for laboratory quality management for the specific test method" (protocol §8.3.1.1), and what evidence of equivalence was accepted. The protocol does not define "equivalent standards".
- The make and model of the elemental analyser, its calibration schedule, and any inter-laboratory comparison. Not stated.
- Whether Isometric's acceptance of the lab is written anywhere as an approved deviation. The Aug 2026 verification report, section 3.5 "Project Deviations and Explanatory Notes (if any)", reads "N/A" (p.15), and the Log of Issues is all "N/A" (p.25); the validation report's 19 issues (pp.16–23) do not mention the lab.
- For Vaulted (both PDDs): the carbon lab is described only as "a laboratory following the required standards" (Great Plains p.14) and "a laboratory, a third-party based in Kansas, following the required standards" (TIRE p.32). Neither PDD states whether that lab is ISO 17025 accredited.
