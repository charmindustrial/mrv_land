# Charm's recurring monthly overhead, mapped to every registry calculation line

Prepared 2026-09-16. Scope: for every emission line / datapoint group in Charm's public calculation tree (`06_charm_registry_footprint.md` §2a/§2b), what the workspace's own instruction files show gets done **every reporting period** — not the one-time PDD design work.

Glossary used below: **BCU** = Book and Claim Unit, a reduction credit Charm applies against certain emissions (diesel, transport) instead of physically eliminating them. **CI** = carbon intensity (emissions per unit of oil). **PP** = Production Period, Charm's internal batch unit for its own pyrolysis. **REC** = Renewable Energy Certificate, used to offset grid-electricity emissions. **BOL** = Bill of Lading, a shipping document. **COBB** = Charm's internal "Basco Injection" tracking tab. **CoA** = Certificate of Analysis (lab report).

Every file path below was opened in full this session. Where the workspace has nothing on a line, I searched named paths and say so.
