## Candidate 1 — Lab-sample courier trips excluded as a zero-marginal-emission activity, not just as sub-materiality

**What NULIFE does.** NULIFE's PDD gives an individual zero-emission argument for shipping test samples by courier, separate from the aggregate 1%-materiality screen. The boundary table's row 25, "Sample Delivery - Injection Site to Nulife" (`03_peer_nulife_ontario_avenue.md` line 114), is marked Excluded outright, with the reasoning quoted verbatim: "Purolator (Canadian road courier) is used for transport, so the transport movement was already taking place regardless... Due to the extremely small mass (< 0.25kg) of the samples, the marginal emissions for moving a package is nearly zero." (PDD p.11, same line). That is a different mechanism from the already-excluded materiality screen (which drops lines only when their aggregate contribution is under 1%) — here the line is argued to be zero regardless of size, because the vehicle trip happens anyway.

**Verifier acceptance.** No line-item verifier quote naming this specific SSR was found in the Verification Report (28 pages, read in full for `03_peer_nulife_ontario_avenue.md` and `08_nulife_lab_acceptance_details.md`, plus pages 11–17 re-read this session). The closest verifier statements are general: "No significant findings that materially impacted the verified net carbon removals nor compliance with protocol standard were identified." (Verification Report p.20, quoted in `08_nulife_lab_acceptance_details.md` line 22) and "Review and confirmation of calculations for total bio-oil carbon stored, CO₂e removals, project activity emissions, project establishment and EOL emissions, uncertainty deductions, and all supporting evidence associated with the reported removals." (Verification Report p.17, read this session). No specific line naming "sample delivery" or "Purolator" appears in the Appendix I issue log (all "N/A," p.25, `08_nulife_lab_acceptance_details.md` line 24). So: general acceptance of the PDD's boundary as verified, no item-level quote.

**What Charm does instead.** Charm includes and calculates this line every period. Charm's boundary table row 43 reads: "Transport Emissions from Transport of Test Samples to Lab" — Included — "Emissions are calculated for air shipment of samples based on a conservative estimate of the point-to-point distance between the sampling site and labs in Google maps." (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 145). Charm's own conservatism note on the same line: "Road distances are used despite the transport assumption being air freight." (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 295).

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Not evidenced. `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md` states its own scope as "what the workspace's own instruction files show gets done every reporting period" (`12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md` line 3), and its per-line table has no row for sample-shipping transport — it is not named as a tracked artifact, checklist item, or named failure point anywhere in that file. I can name the PDD boundary line and its stated calculation method, but cannot point to a named recurring task, spreadsheet, or checklist item in the overhead file that Charm would stop running if it adopted NULIFE's exclusion.

## Candidate 2 — Injection-site electricity calculated from partner-supplied pump specs, not metered

**What NULIFE does.** Injection-site power use is not measured with a meter Charm/NULIFE reads; it is calculated from data the injection partner hands over: "Pump details provided by the Injection Partner. Details provided include pump horsepower and number of hours of operation to inject the relevant batch of material. The details are used to convert energy usage into kWh." (PDD p.25, `03_peer_nulife_ontario_avenue.md` line 103, boundary row 14). Conservatism substitutes for metering precision: "At the injection site, a 20% increase in electricity usage is assumed due to the lack of primary measured data from the injection site." (PDD p.48, same line).

**Verifier acceptance.** No line naming "pump horsepower" or "injection electricity" method appears in the Verification Report's issue log or findings sections read this session. General acceptance only: "Verification of electricity consumption associated with bio-oil production, handling, storage, and injection activities and recalculation of related emissions." (Verification Report p.17, read this session) and the same "No significant findings" line (p.20) cited above. The verified removal shows this line's contribution as part of "Activities 1.709 tCO₂e" (Verification Report p.16, read this session) with no item breakout in the public documents.

**What Charm does instead.** Charm meters injection-site electricity directly at Vaulted. The Section 5a monitoring table's "Vaulted electricity" row gives frequency "each injection, reported monthly," instrument/source "The reading on both meters is noted by site staff prior to and following injection," and evidence produced "monthly MRV Report" (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 216). The corresponding boundary-table entry: "34. Fuel Use from Injection (Vaulted) — Included — 'monthly report on energy usage specific to Charm emplacements … corrected for fuel pump uncertainty'" (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 136) — a fuel line, quoted here because it names the same monthly Vaulted report that also carries the electricity reading; the electricity line itself is row 32, "Electricity Use from Injection (Vaulted) — Included — operator-reported: 'The Well Operator is required to provide a report on energy usage specific to Charm emplacements.'" (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 134).

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Not evidenced. `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md` has no per-line row for "injection-site electricity" or "Vaulted electricity meter reading" — the file's per-line table covers AECN transport, B6 diesel/gasoline, B6 embodied, BCU adjustment lines, Charm production emissions, waste disposal, and REC-inventory monitoring, but not a Vaulted-electricity-metering task. I cannot name a specific recurring artifact or step this would remove.
