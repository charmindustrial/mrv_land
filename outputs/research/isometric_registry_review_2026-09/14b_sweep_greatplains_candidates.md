## Candidate 1 — Fixed per-tonne site embodied-emissions factor, set once, versus a monthly rebuilt inventory workbook

**What Great Plains does (verbatim quote + document + page).**
PDD, Appendix 13: GHG Statement Information, p.75–76 (as quoted in `04_peer_vaulted_great_plains.md` §8.4, sourced to the PDD read this session): "Materials were allocated to each metric ton of waste based on a conservative estimate of lifetime processing waste capacity of 2,000,000 metric tons of waste." The registry sensitivity table gives the resulting factors (PDD p.15): "Site material: steel — Estimated by the site manager — 3.5E-05 ton"; "Site material: concrete — Calculated based on actual data provided by the site manager — 7.7E-05 m3". No embodied-emissions node appears in the per-removal calculation tree for `rmv_1M0ACG4FJ1S02V0A` (confirmed in `04_peer_vaulted_great_plains.md` §6) — the factor is applied as a standing constant, not rebuilt per removal or per period.

**Verifier acceptance (verbatim quote + page, or "none found").**
The Verification Report's stated verification type includes "Embodied Emissions Accounting v1.0" (VR p.4, quoted in `04_peer_vaulted_great_plains.md` §2) as one of the modules under which this project was verified. Table 4, "Prior Findings" (VR p.17, heading read directly this session), lists exactly three open FAR rows, all on gas-detection/migration monitoring — "Recurring gas excursions were observed but compositional follow-up sampling was not consistently undertaken or documented for each relevant event"; "Multiple daily gas release events / alarm excursions occurred without documented investigation of cause and without timely notification to the VVB"; "Verified gas detections / excursions occurred and were potentially not handled in line with permit reporting and investigation requirements" — none of the three concerns embodied emissions. Table 5, "Exemptions From Isometric" (VR p.18, read directly this session), likewise carries no row naming embodied emissions or the steel/concrete factor. The site-manager-estimated, lifetime-capacity-amortized factor is therefore not flagged as a compliance gap anywhere in the 32-page report. Charm's own PDD independently treats Vaulted's identical fixed-factor method as acceptable practice: it quotes and adopts it for its own Kansas removals (see next paragraph), which is itself evidence the method is Isometric-workable.

**What Charm does instead (verbatim quote with location).**
For the Kansas site, Charm's PDD adopts Vaulted's fixed factor directly: "Vaulted … reported the embodied emissions associated with the injection equipment on their site divided by the estimated well capacity to arrive at a quantity of embodied emissions per emplaced tonne of material." Appendix 6 gives the values: "0.0000351566 tons of steel and 0.000077 m3 of concrete per ton of waste injected, given their conservative estimate of a 2 million metric ton overall well capacity." (Both sentences verbatim from `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 138, echoing `knowledge-base/pdd_full.txt` lines 643 and 2531.)

For Basco 6, the PDD's own boundary table leaves the equivalent row blank and delegates it: "37. Embodied Emissions from Injection Equipment (Charm) — blank — Supplemental Doc 3." (`02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 139; row 37 of the boundary table). What actually happens each period is a rebuilt workbook: "Basco Injection Embodied Emissions | 1.426 tCO₂e | Reported | Basco 6 Capital and Sitework Embodied Emissions Inventory July 1 - July 31.xlsx" (`06_charm_registry_footprint.md` line 187) — a new, dated, per-period inventory file, not a standing factor. `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md`'s per-line table confirms the recurring cost: "Monthly-named inventory workbook, rebuilt every period; LCA docx per new CapEx item" and names an open verifier finding against exactly this line — "ISS-36" (Basco site embodied-emissions allocation approach) with resolution "Updated amortization plan proposed and under review. To be implemented from August 2026 RP forward" (`12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md` row "B6 Injection — Embodied Emissions").

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.**
Named artifact from `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md`: the monthly-named "Basco 6 Capital and Sitework Embodied Emissions Inventory [dates].xlsx" rebuild, plus the associated QA sub-task, named in the same file as site-emissions-qa/SKILL.md's "Mandatory Sub-Task 3 (LCA documentation verification, "for every item added this RP")". A fixed per-tonne Basco embodied factor, set once against Basco's estimated capacity (PDD: "Estimated range: 465,000 to 840,000 MT," `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md` line 81) the way Vaulted's is set against its 2,000,000 t lifetime capacity, would replace the monthly inventory-workbook rebuild and its LCA-per-new-item sub-task with a single constant applied every period — directly the artifact and QA step ISS-36's own "updated amortization plan" is already reaching toward.

## Candidate 2 — Verifying against draft (pre-final-sign-off) regulator monitoring data, as an Isometric-standard waiver

**What Great Plains does (verbatim quote + document + page).**
Verification Report, Table 5 "Exemptions From Isometric," p.18: "In accordance with Isometric policy, this verification was performed using draft monitoring reports substantiated by primary data records (e.g., sensor data, weigh tickets and DAS logs), as final regulatory sign-off is not a prerequisite for completing a verification. Should a regulator subsequently reject data or removals contained within these draft reports, Isometric's protocol guardrails require the supplier to compensate for the exact CO2e value of the affected removals through subsequent verification adjustments or the buffer pool." The row's "Standard" column cites "Isometric Standard Sections 3.9, 4.2, and 5.6"; the "Waiver, Exemption or Not Applicable" column reads "Waiver."

**Verifier conclusion.** The same passage (VR p.18) states the conclusion directly: verification proceeded on draft data because "as final regulatory sign-off is not a prerequisite for completing a verification." It names a fallback if a regulator later rejects the data: "Should a regulator subsequently reject data or removals contained within these draft reports, Isometric's protocol guardrails require the supplier to compensate for the exact CO2e value of the affected removals through subsequent verification adjustments or the buffer pool." The waiver does not relax the eventual correctness bar, only the timing.

**What Charm does instead (verbatim quote with location).** Not evidenced. No passage in `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md`, `11_charm_pdd_change_surface.md`, or `knowledge-base/pdd_full.txt` (grepped for "wait for," "pending regulatory," "final sign-off," "draft," "prerequisite," "Office of Conservation," "LDNR" in this context) states that Charm's verification for Basco 6 is held for final Louisiana Office of Conservation / LDNR sign-off, nor that it proceeds on draft data. Charm's PDD does reference LDNR-facing regulatory filings on a fixed timeline (e.g., Form UIC-23/UIC-24 for well closure, `knowledge-base/pdd_full.txt` lines 348, 362), but nothing found ties Charm's periodic GHG Statement verification timing to those filings one way or the other.

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Not evidenced as a current cost — this is flagged as a candidate rather than a confirmed savings because no source shows Charm currently waiting on final regulatory sign-off before a period can be verified. If Charm's practice turns out to mirror the wait Isometric Standard Sections 3.9/4.2/5.6 otherwise allow it to skip, the fix is to request the same standing waiver Vaulted has (verify on draft primary-data records, true up later if a regulator rejects something) rather than holding a reporting period open until Louisiana paperwork is finalized. This would need a direct read of Charm's own verification reports for evidence of an actual wait before it can be stated as a savings, which was outside this sweep's source list.
