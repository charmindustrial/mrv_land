## Candidate 1 — Mass measured off a supplier-side weigh ticket; no scale owned or maintained at the storage site

**What TIRE does.** GHG Statement Report, RP1, `tire_ghg1.pdf` p.2 (machine text, verbatim): "Biosolids truck loads are weighed at an external facility using calibrated equipment, and the recorded weights are provided to us regularly along with supporting documentation. No scale is available on site to perform truck weighing." The same document, p.4, describes the calculation approach: "biosolids transportation (CNG consumption based on distance and truck efficiency)" and lists "onsite energy use (grid electricity and propane)" separately — mass itself is not re-measured at TIRE.

Indirect description (image page, not machine text): PDD p.24 (`tire_pdd.pdf`, read this session as a page image), in the B.14 sensitivity table's "Truck load" row, states that truck loads are measured at the biosolids provider's scale and that the associated uncertainty note discusses annual scale calibration and flags that calibration was not independently confirmed for this arrangement. Because this page has no extractable text layer, I am not presenting it as a verbatim quote.

**Verifier acceptance.** None found. The verification report page read this session (`tire_ver2.pdf` p.19, "Appendix 2: Files Reviewed," page image) lists several calibration-certificate files among the documents reviewed, but no document description on that page addresses mass-measurement location or method, and no sentence I could read states the verifier reviewed or endorsed off-site weighing.

**What Charm does instead.** `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md`, §4: "Mass is monitored by Scale Tickets provided by the injection well personnel. / These values are measured by weighing the delivery truck prior to and after injection on a certified scale" and "The mass of consumables added to the bio-oil being processed are subtracted from the Total Injectate Mass to compute the Mass of Bio-Oil Injected".

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Not evidenced. `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md`'s row "Bio-oil injection — sequestration (Mass of Injectant, Carbon Content of Bio-oil Injected)" lists recurring artifacts as "full/empty ticket or tote weighing log; CoA lab report; Ops Notes with cell-comment annotations" and checklist items testing mass and carbon per batch. Scale ownership or calibration is not named as a recurring task or burden anywhere in that file. With no verifier acceptance on TIRE's side and no named Charm overhead line tied to scale ownership, this candidate is recorded as checked and ruled out, not proposed.

## Candidate 2 — A short GHG Statement Report that refers back to the PDD instead of restating it every period

**What TIRE does.** The GHG Statement Report template, `tire_ghg1.pdf` p.1 (machine text, verbatim, identical wording repeated on `tire_ghg2.pdf` p.1): "After this document has been completed for the first time, it may be acceptable to refer back to relevant sections for subsequent Reporting Periods, for example if there have been no changes to the GHG Assessment scope, data collection methodology, calculation methodology or assumptions." TIRE used the permission: the Data section of the second report, `tire_ghg2.pdf` p.2, reads in full: "No changes have been made since the last submission period." The first report's Results section, `tire_ghg1.pdf` p.5, closes: "Please refer to the TIRE PDD for a detailed explanation of assumptions and choices made." `tire_ghg1.pdf` is 5 pages; `tire_ghg2.pdf` is 4 pages (page 4 blank).

**Verifier acceptance.** Indirect (image page, not machine text): `tire_ver2.pdf` p.19 lists a file named "02-21-26_07-10-26 Isometric GHG Statement Report (3)" among the documents reviewed for a verification report dated August 28, 2026 and versioned V1.1 — i.e., a completed verification round reviewed the short, refer-back-style report and no finding on that page objects to its length or structure. I am not quoting the file list verbatim since the page has no extractable text layer.

**What Charm does instead.** `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md`, §1 and §9: Charm's GHG Statement Report is "Appendix 6: GHG Statement Report," 16,191 computed words. §9 item 1 describes it: "Roughly 30 emission lines, each written out four times (Data Source; Data Quality Assessment; Calculation Methodology; Justification; Unit/Frequency; Modifications to Address Uncertainty). Most of the boilerplate on GLEC, GREET, ATRI, EPA "reputable source" status is repeated per line."

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Named finding from `06_charm_registry_footprint.md`, §3, quoting Charm's own most recent GHG Statement Report: "Charm's integration of dMRV functionality for our CO production facility is not yet complete… Emissions for Charm oil have been computed and reported using the same spreadsheet-based method as outlined in the previous GHG Statement Report." The same file's §1b table shows Charm's periodic report already uses a refer-back pattern in its calculation-narrative cells ("Please see corresponding PDD Appendix"), but the PDD's underlying Appendix 6 that it refers back to is still the full 16,191-word document described above. Trimming Appendix 6 itself to a shorter baseline statement, with a stated refer-back convention for unchanged periods (as TIRE's template permits and TIRE used), targets the specific manual-rebuild burden `06_charm_registry_footprint.md` documents in Charm's own words.

## Candidate 3 — Fuel-based (mpg) transport calculation instead of a distance-based method with route-map evidence

**What TIRE does.** GHG Statement Report, `tire_ghg1.pdf` p.4 (machine text, verbatim): "Operational emissions were calculated using facility data and lifecycle databases, covering onsite energy use (grid electricity and propane), biosolids transportation (CNG consumption based on distance and truck efficiency), high-pressure equipment operation, diesel use for foreign material handling, pump maintenance, end-of-life (EoL) equipment impacts, and lab sampling transport."

Indirect description (image page, not machine text): PDD pp.23-24 (`tire_pdd.pdf`, page image) present, in the B.14 sensitivity table, a fuel-use row sourced as measured mpg provided by the transport company and a distance row sourced from a road-mileage lookup between the supplier and the well site, both treated as low-uncertainty fixed inputs rather than a distance-based, per-shipment calculation. Because I am relying on a page image, these figures are described, not quoted.

**Verifier acceptance.** None found. `tire_ver2.pdf` p.19 (page image) lists the files reviewed for the V1.1 verification round; no listed document description addresses the transport-calculation method itself.

**What Charm does instead.** `02_charm_pdd_baseline.md`, §3, boundary row 12: "Fuel Use from Feedstock Transport – Charm — Included — calculated: "Distance Based Method outlined in the Isometric Transportation Emissions Accounting module, using scale tickets to document the mass transported. Additional fuel used in feedstock handling is calculated per ton of feedstock based on an average fuel consumption factor for lumber handling"." §3 also gives the routing rule applied throughout: "When multiple routes are presented, the longest route is assumed to ensure a conservative estimate of removals".

**What Charm would stop doing or do faster.** Named artifact from `12_charm_recurring_overhead_by_line.md`, row "Bio-oil transport — AECN→SOPOR, SOPOR→Opelousas, Opelousas→Basco": "route-map PNGs" as a recurring per-batch artifact, tested by "items 3.1–3.7 (BOL/transport, 7 items)," with the row's own documented recurring failure: "the Douglas Tank PNG showed 1,759 mi while the calc sheet used 1,729 mi (30 mi gap caught only after opening the PNG)." A fuel-based method would remove the need to source, store, and QA a route-map PNG per leg and would remove that specific named failure mode. Caveat: I did not re-open TIRE's live registry entry this session to check whether TIRE's own PDD distance figure is internally consistent with what the registry actually applies, so this trade is directional, not fully verified in this pass.
