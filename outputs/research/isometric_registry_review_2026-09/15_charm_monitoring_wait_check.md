# Does Charm's Isometric verification wait on storage-site monitoring data or regulator sign-off before certificates issue?

**Verdict: Yes** — Charm's own August 2025 submission was delayed by Vaulted's monitoring-data turnaround, and Isometric/350Solutions have repeatedly carried unresolved monitoring items as open Forward Action Requests (FARs) rather than blocking crediting for the current period — but at least one round was explicitly gated on getting a formal exemption from Isometric before the verifier would issue credits. Regulator sign-off itself (LDNR, KDHE) is not shown as a gating step — Charm submits regulator-facing permit reports on its own compliance timeline, and the verifier reviews monitoring data, not a regulator sign-off letter.

### 8. Regulator sign-off (LDNR/KDHE) itself — not shown as a gating step

Verification Report page 6 (read this session): "Charm pretreats the bio-oil via air sparging... to ensure bio-oil and aqueous product meet the requirements of the injection facility permit for storage in permeable reservoirs as specified by the Louisiana Department of Energy & Natural Resources (LDNR)." Page 15, row "3.1.3 Injection Pressure": "Pressure is continuously monitored and reported quarterly (monitoring reports indicate full compliance) for Basco site" — the verifier reviews Charm's own compliance monitoring data and permit-required reports, not a discrete regulator "sign-off" document, and no source read or searched in this session shows a verification round waiting on an LDNR or KDHE approval letter, certificate, or closure sign-off as a precondition to certificate issuance. `kdhe-kora-request/SKILL.md` (read this session) describes Charm's Kansas monitoring evidence as periodic KORA-requested reports pulled from KDHE on Charm's own initiative — a records request, not a wait for regulator approval.
