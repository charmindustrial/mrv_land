Sensitivity analysis
Submissions
Datapoints are varied individually by +/- 20% as a conservative standard and net CO₂e recalculated. Certify skips a contribution analysis, instead including all datapoints in the sensitivity analysis unless they are list inputs related to CO₂ storage or GHG reduction which are assumed to be accurate primary data and can be omitted. If varying a datapoint by +/- 20% causes a <1% change to net CO₂e removal no further action is required, but if the change is >1% the datapoint is considered sensitive. Sensitive datapoint's uncertainty must be accounted for with an uncertainty justification.
