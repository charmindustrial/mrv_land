Vaulted geologically sequestered 13,326.56 US tons of waste during this audit period. This is the equivalent of 4,061.55 metric tons of gross CO2 is permanently removed from the atmosphere. Associated with this activity emissions occurred, including:
• 43.191 tons of CO2 emissions from energy consumption on site (processing & injection)
• 244.963 tons of CO2 emissions from transportation to facility
• 38.737 tons of CO2 emissions from diesel consumption on site
• 0.32281 tons of CO2 emissions from MRV lab sampling
• 1.799 tons of CO2 from replacement emissions
• 114.665 tons of CO2 from screening emissions at supplier's site
• 6.996 tons CO2 emissions from historical cavern propane (CH4,CO2)
Together, a total of 450.67 tons of CO2e were emitted due to the carbon removal activities.
This resulted in a total of 3,610.88 net tons of CO2e removed from the atmosphere.
